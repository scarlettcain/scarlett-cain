branding

[ cookout rebrand ]

Spring 2026

Rebrand of NC fast food chain Cook OUt. Focus on rebranding current packaging of the restaurant and creating Cook Out branded kitchenware for home.

[ poxxy band ]

Spring 2026

Group project with the prompt to make a branding design for a band. This CD, billboard, and advertisement are my contributions to the branding for one of three albums.
Each album had an individual branding and style while still fitting within the overall "Poxxy" band branding guidelines we created.

[ patterns ]

Spring 2026

This project had 3 parts: creating a pattern representing me as an individual, a pattern representing the future, and a socially conscious pattern.

The personal pattern is in red, taking inspiration from floral lace and horses.

The future pattern is the bitmapped video format, and takes inspiration from digital iconography.

The socially conscious pattern was a group project where we made a quilt reading "Be Kind" and put it up in a Boston T (subway/ trolley) station in order to get people to notice their surroundings and be kind on public transport.
