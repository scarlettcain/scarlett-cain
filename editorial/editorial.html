[ what is.. ]

What is Graphic Design? Who is a Graphic Designer?
Spring 2026 - Sophomore Graphic Design

64-page book answering the question: What is Graphic Design? Who is a Graphic Designer? The final is a 6x9in saddle-stitched book with four signatures. Bodycopy was formatted like a conversation, with sections describing main themes of the book. Each section had a different color palette taken from the cover. Experimentation with colored typography, a serif bodycopy with a sans-serif header, and an exposed grid on the cover.
