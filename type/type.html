typography

[ zodiac book ]

6x9 in - Fall 2025

Booklet for the zodiac sign Libra. Each page has the same title, dates, and bodycopy that was evaluated 20 different ways. The final booklet is a 6x9 spiral bound french folded book.

[ 60s cars booklet ]

4.25x5.5in - Spring 2026

Booklet describing Iconic Classic Cars of the 1960s, The prompt of this project was to design a pamphlet on colored paper. Final Booklet is a 24 page 4.25x5.5in (hand-sewn) singer saddle-stitched booklet

[ do you have worms for brains ]

Digital - Spring 2026

Motion poster using analog typography. The phrase "do you have worms for brains" was typed on a typewriter in seven different pixelated fonts, using an X as a pixel. Papers were scanned in and then formatted in Adobe AfterEffects alongside adding the worm background. Final poster was projected at 24x36in.
